================================================================================
SAFETY LAYERS
================================================================================

Layer 1: Data QA & Circuit Breaker
         └─ Validates data quality, halts if unsafe

Layer 2: Signal Agent Confidence Threshold
         └─ Must have ≥65% confidence to proceed

Layer 3: Risk Management Rules
         └─ Position sizing, limits, diversification

Layer 4: Policy & Self-Critic Review
         └─ AI critical review, blocks if uncertain

Layer 5: Execution Safeguards
         └─ Order validation, price checks

Layer 6: Position Management
         └─ Stop losses, take profits, trailing stops

Layer 7: Portfolio Limits
         └─ Daily trade limit, loss limit, position limit
